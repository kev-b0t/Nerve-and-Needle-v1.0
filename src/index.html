Roots follow PM&R Recap where it lists the muscle, otherwise the MRC Aids to the Examination of the Peripheral Nervous System, with surgical-EMG data (Levin 1996, Tsao 2003). Where sources disagree, the alternatives are shown and accepted. Needle sites are paraphrased from Perotto, Anatomical Guide for the Electromyographer, 4th ed., with page numbers.
